1. Getting Started

Welcome to the Addis Ababa Tourism Commission Portal!

This guide will help you register your establishment and manage your business profile online.

What You'll Be Able To Do:

  • Register your tourism establishment online.
  • Complete detailed information about your facilities and services.
  • Upload required business documents and certificates.
  • Track your registration status in real-time.
  • Update your establishment profile anytime.
Tip: Bookmark the portal URL for quick access. We recommend using a desktop or laptop for the best experience when filling out detailed sections.

2. System Overview

Who Should Use This System?

This portal is designed for establishment owners and managers in the tourism industry, including:

Hotels & Boutique

Restaurants & F&B

Meeting Halls

Conference Centers

MICE Facilities

Other Tourism Businesses

System Requirements

Supported Browsers:

  • Google Chrome (latest version)
  • Microsoft Edge (latest version)
  • Mozilla Firefox (latest version)
  • Safari (latest version)

For best results, use the latest version of your browser and maintain a stable internet connection.

3. Login & Account Access

Logging In

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
  2. Enter the portal URL provided by the commission office.
  3. Enter your username and password on the login page.
  4. Check "Remember me" only if you are on your own personal device.
  5. Click "Sign In" to access your Dashboard.
First-Time Login: Your username and password will be sent via SMS. The default password is Default Pass. We recommend changing this password after your first login.

4. Pre-Registration Process

Not yet registered? Follow these steps to create your initial account:

  1. Click "No account? Pre-register here" on the login page.
  2. Enter your establishment name (trade name).
  3. Provide your Tax Identification Number (TIN).
  4. Enter a valid phone number (this will receive your login credentials via SMS).
  5. Provide your email address.
  6. Select your business category (Hotel, Restaurant, MICE Venue, etc.).
  7. Verify with OTP – enter the One-Time Password sent via SMS.
  8. Click "Submit" to complete pre-registration.

What Happens Next? The system will create your establishment record (pending status), send login credentials via SMS, and generate an admin account for your business.

5. Your Dashboard

Your Dashboard is your command center. Here you will see all establishments linked to your account and their current status.

Dashboard Overview

For each establishment, you will see:

  • Trade Name: The name your customers know you by.
  • Registration Status: Pending, Approved, or other status.
  • Business Category: Your type of establishment.
  • Location: City and address information.
  • Star Rating: Your establishment's star classification.
  • Action Buttons: View, Edit, or Manage Documents.

What You Can Do From the Dashboard: View details, update profiles, upload documents, and track registration status.

6. Managing Your Establishment Profile

Your establishment profile is organized into logical sections. You can complete each section at your own pace—you do not have to fill everything in at once.

6.1 Basic Information

This is the foundation of your profile. Complete this first:

  • Trade Name: Your establishment's name (as customers know it).
  • Registration Name: Your official business registration name.
  • Specific Address: Complete street address.
  • Subcity and City: Administrative location details.
  • Star Category: Your establishment's star rating (1 to 5 stars).
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers and email addresses.
  • Reservation Contact: Direct booking phone number and contact person.

6.2 Accommodation

Detail your room inventory:

  • King Size Rooms: Number of king bed rooms.
  • Twin Bed Rooms: Number of rooms with two separate beds.
  • Junior Suites: Number of junior suite rooms.
  • Full Suites: Number of complete suite accommodations.
  • Presidential Suites: Number of premium suites.
  • Accessible Rooms: Number of wheelchair-accessible rooms.
  • VIP Check-in: Availability of special VIP check-in service.
The system automatically calculates your total rooms and total bed capacity.

6.3 Food and Beverage

Tell us about your dining options:

  • All-Day Dining: Restaurant with continuous service.
  • Specialty Restaurants: Different cuisine types you offer.
  • Coffee Shop: Café or beverage service.
  • Bars and Lounges: Number of bar or lounge areas.
  • Night Club: Evening entertainment venue.
  • Catering Services: Event catering capacity and details.
  • Special Dietary Options: Vegetarian, vegan, halal, or other options.

6.4 Meetings and Events

Register your meeting and event facilities:

  • Room or Hall Name: What you call each space.
  • Room Type: Ballroom, conference room, boardroom, etc.
  • Dimensions: Width, length, and ceiling height.
  • Capacity: Maximum number of people the space can accommodate.
  • Setup Arrangements: Theater style, classroom, banquet, etc.

6.5 Facilities and Amenities

Share what makes your establishment special:

  • Internet: Wi-Fi availability and bandwidth.
  • Pool: Type of pool (indoor, outdoor, or both).
  • Spa and Wellness: Spa services and massage availability.
  • Children's Facilities: Playground, daycare, kids club.
  • Green Spaces: Gardens, outdoor areas.

6.6 Accessibility Features

Describe how you welcome guests with disabilities:

  • Wheelchair Ramps: Accessible entry points.
  • Elevators: Number and type (wheelchair-accessible lifts).
  • Accessible Bathrooms: Public restrooms with accessibility features.

6.7 Safety and Security

Confirm your security measures:

  • CCTV: Video surveillance in public areas.
  • Fire Safety: Last fire extinguisher inspection date, alarms, exits.
  • Smoke Detectors: In rooms and public areas.
  • Security Screening: Bag scanners and walk-through scanners.

6.8 Parking and Transport

Information about transportation:

  • Parking Spaces: Number of covered and uncovered spaces.
  • Parking Services: Valet parking, self-parking.
  • Airport Transport: Shuttle service availability.
  • EV Charging: Electric vehicle charging station availability.

6.9 Technology and ICT

  • POS System: What point-of-sale system you use.
  • Booking System: Online reservation system details.
  • Guest Services: Mobile check-in, digital concierge, etc.

6.10 Staffing and Sustainability

  • Staff: Number of employees and languages spoken.
  • Certifications: Environmental, health, safety, and quality certifications.
  • Green Practices: Sustainability initiatives and waste management.
Tips for Completing Your Profile:
  • Start with Basic Information first.
  • Save each section as you complete it.
  • Be accurate and complete.
  • Use clear, professional descriptions.
  • Update your profile regularly.

7. Uploading Supporting Documents

Required Documents

  • Business License: Trade license or business registration certificate.
  • Tax Certificate: TIN certificate or tax clearance.
  • Property and Facility Photos: Professional photos of exterior, lobby, guest rooms, dining areas, meeting halls, and facilities.
  • Health and Safety Certificates: Food hygiene certificate, health inspection reports.
  • Fire Safety and Inspection: Fire safety inspection certificate, fire extinguisher maintenance records.
  • Environmental Permits: Environmental clearance or waste management permits.

How to Upload Documents

  1. From your dashboard open your establishment profile.
  2. Scroll to the "Attachments" or "Documents" section.
  3. Click "Add Document" or "Upload File".
  4. Select the document category from the dropdown.
  5. Choose the file from your computer.
  6. Click "Upload" and wait for confirmation.

File Requirements

Requirement Details
Accepted Formats PDF, JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
Maximum File Size Check with administrator for size limits
Image Quality Use clear, well-lit photos for best results

8. Best Practices & Tips for Success

To ensure a smooth registration and approval process:

  1. Complete Your Profile Accurately Double-check all information before saving. Provide realistic numbers for rooms, capacity, and amenities. Use professional descriptions.
  2. Submit Required Documents Promptly Upload all required licenses and certificates. Use high-quality, clear scans or photos. Ensure documents are legible and complete.
  3. Keep Your Information Up-to-Date Update your profile when you add facilities or services. Change contact information if phone or email changes. Keep certifications and permits current.
  4. Respond Promptly to Requests Check for messages from the commission office regularly. Provide requested additional information quickly. Ask for clarification if you do not understand a requirement.
  5. Use a Secure Internet Connection Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions. Change your password regularly. Never share your login credentials.

9. Troubleshooting

Having Issues? Here is how to resolve common problems:

  • Login fails or wrong username or password: Verify your credentials. If you forgot your password, contact the System Administrator for a reset.
  • I have not received my OTP code: Wait a few moments. Check your phone number. Request a new OTP code if it still does not arrive.
  • The OTP code is being rejected: The code may have expired (usually after 10 minutes). Request a new OTP and enter it immediately.
  • My profile section will not save: Check your internet connection. Verify all required fields are filled. Try saving one field at a time.
  • File upload is failing: Check the file format (PDF, JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP). Make sure the file size is not too large.
  • The system says my TIN already exists: Your establishment may have been registered before. Contact the commission office with your TIN to retrieve your account.
  • My page keeps refreshing or displays incorrectly: Clear your browser cache and cookies. Try a different browser. Ensure you are using an up-to-date browser version.
Still Having Issues? Contact the System Administrator or the Addis Ababa Tourism Commission office directly. See the Support section below.
