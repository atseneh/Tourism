### Slide 2: Introduction & Vision
*   **Header:** Empowering Ethiopia's Tourism Sector
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   **Vision:** To create a centralized, secure, and real-time digital registry for all tourism and hospitality stakeholders in Addis Ababa.
    *   **Goal:** Streamline registration, improve regulatory oversight, and provide actionable insights through a National Command Center.
    *   **Focus:** "Land of Origins - Tourism Management Portal."

---

### Slide 3: Key Challenges Addressed
*   **Header:** Why Do We Need This Portal?
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   Fragmented data across different tourism sectors.
    *   Manual and time-consuming registration processes for hotels and organizations.
    *   Lack of real-time visibility into infrastructure health and active stakeholders.
    *   Need for secure, role-based access for ministry officials and business owners.

---

### Slide 4: Core Features & Capabilities
*   **Header:** System Overview
*   **Bullet Points:**
    *   **Secure Authentication:** Multi-layered access for Hotel Owners, Commissioners, and System Admins.
    *   **Organization Pre-Registration:** Streamlined onboarding capturing vital data (TIN, Sector Category, Contact Info).
    *   **National Command Center:** Live dashboard providing real-time situational awareness.
    *   **Infrastructure Health Monitoring:** Uptime tracking and data integrity checks.

---

### Slide 6: Role Profile - Hotel Owner
*   **Header:** Empowering Hoteliers
*   **Use Case Functionalities:**
    *   **Pre-Registration & Onboarding:** Submit initial hotel details and required documentation directly through the portal.
    *   **Profile Management:** Update and maintain comprehensive registry data for the establishment.
    *   **Infrastructure Logging:** Catalogue all facility attributes (rooms, amenities, special features) up to 70+ data points.
    *   **Status Tracking:** Monitor real-time approval status and regulatory compliance alerts.
    *   **Communication:** Receive targeted notifications and policy updates from the Ministry.

---

### Slide 7: Role Profile - Ministry Official / Commissioner
*   **Header:** Regulatory Oversight & Insights
*   **Use Case Functionalities:**
    *   **National Dashboard Access:** View live metrics on registered users and active hoteliers via the Command Center.
    *   **Application Review:** Evaluate, approve, or reject pre-registration and infrastructure updates submitted by Hotel Owners.
    *   **System Alert Monitoring:** Track and resolve system-wide alerts and compliance issues flagged by the registry.
    *   **Reporting & Analytics:** Generate detailed reports on regional infrastructure and tourism capacity.

---

### Slide 8: Role Profile - System Administrator
*   **Header:** Technical Health & Security Management
*   **Use Case Functionalities:**
    *   **User & Role Management:** Provision and revoke access rights for all system users (Owners, Commissioners).
    *   **Infrastructure Health Monitoring:** Track uptime and performance of crucial nodes (Registry Cache Node, Auth Gateway).
    *   **Data Integrity Auditing:** Ensure the consistency and security of the centralized tourism data registry.
    *   **System Configuration:** Maintain portal settings and manage internal taxonomy (e.g., Sector Categories, sub-city dropdowns).
